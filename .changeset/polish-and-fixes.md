Polish and fixes. Agents know their teammates by name and id, and add teammates with `agent_create` when you ask; a new Agent's first-run ritual no longer answers a teammate's message. The conversation keeps its place and your unsent text when you go to another Agent and back, and Teams and Agents made from the CLI or by an Agent show up without a reload. Questions asked or answered elsewhere come and go in the open conversation, a finished turn no longer resets the view you are reading, long names no longer push the conversation bar's controls away, the side panel on a narrow screen closes with Escape, a stopped reply says so, and a Team whose folder is not a Git repository no longer logs a failed request.
