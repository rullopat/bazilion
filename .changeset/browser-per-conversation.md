**Which browser, chosen in the conversation.** The browser setting is now the default (an Agent's own browser unless you change it), and you tell an Agent in a conversation to "use my Chrome" or "use your own browser": it switches with the new `browser_use` tool for the rest of that conversation. Moving to your Chrome asks you first, in the conversation and on Telegram, unless your Chrome is already the default; where nobody can answer, it is refused. The Agent's Browser panel names the browser it is using.
