**Search posts on X.** With an xAI key saved under Models, Agents get `x_search`: xAI searches X, reads the posts and answers from them, and the Agent gets the answer, the links of the posts it cites and how many it read (xAI bills by them). It narrows by accounts (`handles` or `exclude_handles`) and by days. **Posts on X** on the Web search card turns it off; **X search model** picks the Grok model (`grok-4.7` by default).
