**The Agent can reread what a summary replaced.** When a long conversation is summarized, the summary is all the Agent reads of its older messages. A new `recall` tool lets it search those messages and read them in full, tool output included, when the summary leaves out a detail it needs: an exact value, a link, your own words. In the conversation the step shows as **Searched older messages for …** or **Reread older messages**.
