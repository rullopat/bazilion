**Your order and width for the list, and messages that show at once.** Drag a Team by its header, or an Agent within its Team, to put the list on the left in your order, or use Move up and Move down in their menus; the order is kept in Bazilion, so every browser, Manage and `bazilion team list` / `agent list` show it, and `bazilion team order` and `bazilion agent order` set it from a terminal. Drag the list's right edge to make it wider or narrower; each browser keeps its width. What arrives in the open conversation from elsewhere, a teammate's message, Telegram, a schedule or a follow-up, now shows at once, also while that turn still runs, and flashes for a moment, instead of waiting behind "Show it"; if you had scrolled up, **New messages ↓** takes you down. A teammate's message shows open, with who wrote it and its text. The upgrade numbers your Teams and Agents in the order they had and keeps everything else as it was.
