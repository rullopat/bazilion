**A tab open across an upgrade now says so, and the list's controls are easier to find.** After an upgrade, a Bazilion tab that stayed open showed the old interface until a full browser reload, and **Retry** on the "Could not load this page" screen kept the old page. Now every answer of the web server says which build it comes from: an older tab shows **Bazilion was updated** with **Reload** (at the latest when its live updates come back after the restart), and **Retry** reloads the whole page. What you typed and did not send is kept for the tab across a reload. In the list on the left, the width handle is always visible as a faint line with a grip at its middle, and a grip shows on a Team or Agent row you point at, to drag it into place.
